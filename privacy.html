Privacy Policy

BLUD Command Center · Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the BLUD Command Center Discord bot (the “Bot”) handles information when members use its registration, guide, event, and progression features. The Bot is operated for the BLUD Kingshot alliance by its server administrators (“we,” “us”).

1. Information we collect

Depending on which features you use, the Bot may process:

  • Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID and the server context in which you use the Bot.
  • Kingshot account details: governor ID, governor name, kingdom number, and the date of registration.
  • Progress statistics: power, Town Center level, timestamps, and the source of each snapshot (manually entered or imported from an authorized CSV).
  • Event information: event names and schedules; your signup response, selected participation role, and attendance-related records if entered by authorized officers.
  • Operational records: reminder-delivery status and CSV import audit information, such as import time, file name, and number of records processed.

The current Bot does not request your Discord password, Kingshot password, payment card details, or your private direct messages. It does not currently run an automatic Kingshot player-data synchronization service.

2. Why we use information

We use this information to link a Discord member to a governor account, provide requested commands and guides, organize events, send configured reminders, show progression reports, and help alliance officers administer BLUD. We do not sell member data or use it for advertising.

3. Where data is stored and who can access it

The current Bot stores its application records in a SQLite database controlled by the server operator. Authorized server administrators may access the underlying database for operations, troubleshooting, or deletion requests. Officer commands are intended to restrict certain reports to designated R4/R5 roles or server administrators. Discord itself processes messages, slash-command interactions, and server activity under Discord's separate policies.

Hosting has not yet been selected. Once deployed, infrastructure providers may process data as necessary to run the Bot. We will update this policy if a new external statistics provider or materially different data flow is enabled.

4. Retention

The current Bot stores linked profiles, historical progression snapshots, and event records until the operator removes them or otherwise retires the service. There is currently no automatic retention or user-facing deletion command. We will review requests to remove or correct a member's records and handle them manually. Some information may remain in Discord messages or independently maintained backups until separately removed.

5. Your choices and requests

For access, correction, unlinking, or deletion of your Bot records, contact the BLUD server owner or an R4/R5 administrator through the BLUD Discord server. Before deploying publicly, the operator must also publish a reliable contact address or support URL: [ADD CONTACT EMAIL OR PUBLIC SUPPORT LINK].

When making a request, include your Discord account and governor ID, but never send passwords or authentication tokens. We may verify ownership before making changes. We will respond within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law.

6. Third-party services

The Bot uses Discord's platform. Other separately installed tools, such as Draeve or Kingshot Project, are independent services with their own data practices. Their inclusion in the BLUD server does not mean this Bot automatically shares databases with them. Future integrations will require separate configuration and review.

7. Security

We aim to limit access to the Bot's data and keep bot tokens and database files out of public code repositories. No service can guarantee absolute security. Please report suspected unauthorized access to the server operator.

8. Children

The Bot is intended for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable law. It is not specifically directed to children below those requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as features, hosting, or data practices change. Updates will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Material changes should be communicated to server members.
